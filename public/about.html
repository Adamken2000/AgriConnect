Transforming Kenyan Agriculture Through Digital Innovation

Agriculture forms the backbone of Kenya's economy, employing over 70% of the rural population. Yet, smallholder farmers often struggle with post-harvest losses and predatory brokers who leverage logistics gaps.

AgriConnect bridges this gap using a modern web architecture. By connecting urban markets, restaurants, and retail buyers directly with farmers in regions like Kinangop, Nakuru, Eldoret, and Meru, we ensure sustainable livelihoods and fresher produce for everyone.
